The Struck Tile

A chamfered brass plaque holding an abbreviated board, the anti-diagonal ignited. Each square is chamfered top-left and bottom-right only, so the facet edges run parallel to the win line — cut corners and lit diagonal are one gesture.

called and callstone are the only two-colour icons — they are the only two that encode state, and they reuse the board's solid and dotted top bars rather than inventing a second metaphor. Undo is the only arc in the set, so it can never be confused with history — an hourglass, not a clock. Close-game is a padlock, never the dismiss ×: one is irreversible, the other isn't.
